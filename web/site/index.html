Tap Dodge Rush

The rules running below are the same compiled engine as the Android build on Google Play — one Java class, compiled to JavaScript by TeaVM, drawn here on a canvas instead of an Android one.

drag, or ← → · space to start

What differs from the Play build. The shipped game sells a continue through a rewarded ad. There are no ads here and free continues would make this score mean something other than the one on your phone, so the web build has none — the engine's canUseContinue() is simply never called.

Get it on Google Play · source · the rules live in :engine, with no Android on its classpath — which is what lets one rulebook drive both builds.
